Safeguarding and parental consent

Most of the students we work with are under 18. These are the rules we follow so that parents always know what is happening and why.

Consent comes first

  • For any student under 18, a parent or legal guardian signs our consent form before an assessment or session.
  • The form explains what we will collect, how we will use it, who will see the report and how long we keep it.
  • We record who gave consent, when, and for which student. A parent can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Students are also asked, in plain words, whether they are happy to take part.

During sessions

  • A parent or guardian joins the opening and closing parts of every session with a student under 18, and can stay throughout.
  • In-person sessions take place at our office at Evita Building, Burma Camp, Dimapur, Nagaland, or on a school campus during school hours.
  • Online sessions are arranged through the parent's phone number or email, never directly with a student under 18.
  • We do not record sessions unless the parent agrees in writing.

When a student needs more than career guidance

We are career counsellors, not clinicians. If a student tells us something that suggests they need medical or psychological support, or that they may be at risk, we tell the parent (unless doing so would put the student at greater risk) and help the family find appropriate professional support.

Reports and data

  • Reports are shared only with the student and their parents, and with a school counsellor only when the parents agree.
  • We never publish a student's name, photo, results or testimonial without written consent.
  • Details of how we handle personal data are in our Privacy notice.

Raising a concern

If you have any concern about how a session was run or how information was handled, contact us at leadcounsellor@careerhubnagaland.in or +91 93660 92897. We reply within 7 days.
