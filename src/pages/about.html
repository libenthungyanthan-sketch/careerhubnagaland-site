About Career Hub Nagaland

Career Hub Nagaland is a career counselling practice in Dimapur for students in Grades 8–12 and graduates. It is an initiative of ICATAN, founded in 2025 and led by Dr. Emmanuel L. Yanthan.

The counsellor

Dr. Emmanuel L. Yanthan is the Lead Career Counsellor at Career Hub Nagaland and a Global Career Counsellor certified through Univariety and UCLA Extension.

He administers and interprets every assessment himself, so the person who reads your child's results is the person who talks them through with you.

Credentials

  • Global Career Counsellor, Univariety and UCLA Extension
  • Lead Career Counsellor, Career Hub Nagaland

How we work

Evidence before advice

Every recommendation starts from a structured assessment on an established platform, Bodhami or Univariety, rather than from opinion or trends.

Career and study skills together

A clear direction helps only if the student can work towards it, so we coach study habits alongside the career plan.

Families in the room

For students under 18, parents give consent and join the key conversations. Decisions stay with the family.

What we do not do

  • We do not guarantee admissions, marks or jobs.
  • We do not diagnose. We are not clinicians; when a student needs clinical support, we say so and help the family find it.
  • We do not sell or share student data with advertisers.
