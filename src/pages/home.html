Each year of school brings its own decision

Most families feel the pressure only in Class 10 or 12. Planning a year ahead turns each of these moments into an informed choice rather than a rushed one.

  3. Class10Choose a streamScience, Commerce or Arts, chosen on evidence rather than trends.
  4. Class11Plan entrancesWhich exams, which dates, and a realistic preparation plan.
  5. Class12Apply with confidenceCollege shortlists, applications and scholarships.

Hard work matters. Effort without a plan is a risk.

"Just study harder" often leads to burnout and second-guessing. A plan built around your child gives that effort a direction.

What often happens

  • Cramming before exams, which raises anxiety and fades quickly.
  • A stream chosen because friends chose it, or because of a trend.
  • Entrance and college forms rushed in late Class 12.

What we help you do instead

  • Study methods built on retrieval practice and spaced revision, both with strong research support.
  • A structured assessment of aptitude and interests before the stream decision.
  • A year-by-year plan for entrances, applications and scholarships.

Three things we work on with your child

Strengths and career direction

Psychometric and aptitude assessments, read with you by the counsellor, then matched to streams, subjects and careers worth exploring.

Study habits and focus

Practical weekly routines: active recall, spaced revision and a realistic timetable that fits your child's school and coaching schedule.

Profile and applications

Projects, writing and leadership activities that show who your child is, and help with college and scholarship applications when the time comes.

How the assessment works

  1. Book a session

    Choose a time online or on WhatsApp. A parent signs the consent form before anything else.

  2. Your child takes the online assessment

    About 30 minutes, on Bodhami or Univariety. It covers aptitude, interests, personality and learning style.

  3. We read the results together

    The counsellor explains what the results mean with you and your child, and answers your questions.

  4. You receive a written report

    Within 24 hours, digital and printed, with recommended streams, subjects and next steps.

What an assessment can and cannot tell you. It shows your child's strengths and interests as they are today, and it structures the decision. It does not predict marks, guarantee an admission or choose a career for anyone. That decision stays with your family; we help you make it with better information.
