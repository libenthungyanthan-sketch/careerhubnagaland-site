Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal data we collect, why we need it, who we share it with and how you can control it. It is written for parents, students and schools in India and follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Who we are

Career Hub Nagaland, an initiative of ICATAN, Evita Building, Burma Camp, Dimapur, Nagaland. For any privacy question, write to emmanuelyanthan@rediffmail.com with the subject "Privacy", or call +91 93660 92897.

2. What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, WhatsApp number, email if given, the student's class and board, your main concern and preferred format.
  • Sessions and assessments: the student's name, school, assessment responses and results, and notes from sessions.
  • Payments: the amount, date and payment reference. Card and bank details are handled by the payment provider; we do not see or store them.
  • Messages: WhatsApp messages and emails you send us.
  • Website: basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited. If we add analytics, we will ask for your consent through a cookie banner first.

3. Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, arrange and deliver assessments and counselling, prepare reports, take payment and keep records we are required to keep. If you tick the separate opt-in, we also send occasional tips and session dates on WhatsApp. We do not sell personal data or share it with advertisers.

4. Children's data

We process the personal data of a student under 18 only with the verifiable consent of a parent or legal guardian, recorded on our signed consent form before any assessment. We do not track children for advertising or build behavioural profiles of them. See our Safeguarding and consent page.

5. Who processes data for us

  • Assessment platforms (Bodhami and Univariety), which hold assessment responses and results under their own data terms.
  • Our website host (Hostinger), which stores enquiry form submissions.
  • Our payment provider, which processes payments.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), which carries our messages.

Each receives only what it needs for its part of the service.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted after 12 months. Records of an engagement, including reports and consent forms, are kept for 3 years after the last session, unless the law requires longer, and then deleted. Before we delete data because its purpose is over, we let you know at least 48 hours in advance.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you or your child, correct it, have it erased, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to act for you. Write to us at the address above. We reply within 30 days.

8. Grievances

Our grievance contact is the Lead Career Counsellor, at emmanuelyanthan@rediffmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Security and breaches

We limit access to student data to the counsellor and the staff who need it, and use password-protected accounts and secure links to share reports. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you without delay, along with what happened and what we are doing about it.
