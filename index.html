Each year of school brings its own decision

Most families feel the pressure only in Class 10 or 12. Planning a year ahead turns each of these moments into an informed choice rather than a rushed one.

  1. Class8Discover strengthsAn early look at aptitude and interests, without exam pressure.
  2. Class9Build study habitsWeekly planning, active recall and spaced revision.
  3. Class10Choose a streamScience, Commerce or Arts, chosen on evidence rather than trends.
  4. Class11Plan entrancesWhich exams, which dates, and a realistic preparation plan.
  5. Class12Apply with confidenceCollege shortlists, applications and scholarships.

Hard work matters. Effort without a plan is a risk.

"Just study harder" often leads to burnout and second-guessing. A plan built around your child gives that effort a direction.

What often happens

  • Cramming before exams, which raises anxiety and fades quickly.
  • A stream chosen because friends chose it, or because of a trend.
  • Entrance and college forms rushed in late Class 12.

What we help you do instead

  • Study methods built on retrieval practice and spaced revision, both with strong research support.
  • A structured assessment of aptitude and interests before the stream decision.
  • A year-by-year plan for entrances, applications and scholarships.

Three things we work on with your child

Strengths and career direction

Psychometric and aptitude assessments, read with you by the counsellor, then matched to streams, subjects and careers worth exploring.

Study habits and focus

Practical weekly routines: active recall, spaced revision and a realistic timetable that fits your child's school and coaching schedule.

Profile and applications

Projects, writing and leadership activities that show who your child is, and help with college and scholarship applications when the time comes.

How the assessment works

  1. Book a session

    Choose a time online or on WhatsApp. A parent signs the consent form before anything else.

  2. Your child takes the online assessment

    About 30 minutes, on Bodhami or Univariety. It covers aptitude, interests, personality and learning style.

  3. We read the results together

    The counsellor explains what the results mean with you and your child, and answers your questions.

  4. You receive a written report

    Within 24 hours, digital and printed, with recommended streams, subjects and next steps.

What an assessment can and cannot tell you. It shows your child's strengths and interests as they are today, and it structures the decision. It does not predict marks, guarantee an admission or choose a career for anyone. That decision stays with your family; we help you make it with better information.

About the counsellor and our approach

Questions parents ask

How much does it cost?

A career assessment with interpretation is ₹3,500. A 60-minute one-to-one counselling session is ₹6,500. Online group sessions are ₹1,200 per student. Every fee is listed on the Fees page; the first 15-minute parent call is free.

How long does the assessment take, and what does the report contain?

The online assessment takes about 30 minutes. It covers aptitude, interests, personality and learning style. The counsellor reads the results with you and your child, and you receive a written report within 24 hours, in digital and printed form.

Is Class 8 or 9 too early to start?

No. In Classes 11 and 12, board exams make calm exploration difficult. Classes 8 to 10 are the best window to build study habits and discover natural strengths without deadline pressure.

How is this different from tuition or coaching?

Tuition teaches syllabus content. We work on how your child learns, how they plan their week, and which direction suits them, so school and coaching become more effective.

Who is the counsellor?

Sessions are led by Dr. Emmanuel L. Yanthan, Lead Career Counsellor and Global Career Counsellor certified through Univariety and UCLA Extension. Read more about him.

Is the assessment scientifically valid? What can it not tell us?

The assessments run on established Indian career platforms (Bodhami and Univariety) and are interpreted by the counsellor. They show your child's current strengths and interests. They do not predict marks, guarantee admission or decide a career; they help you make that decision with better information.

Do parents attend?

Yes. We ask a parent or guardian to join the first and last parts of every session for students under 18, and to sign the consent form before any assessment.

Is it in person or online?

Both. We meet families in person at our office in Evita Building, Burma Camp, Dimapur, and online for families elsewhere in Nagaland and the North-East.

What happens to my child's data?

We collect only what we need, use it only to deliver the service, and never sell or share it with advertisers. Reports are shared only with the parent and student. Details are in our Privacy notice.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

You can reschedule once free of charge up to 24 hours before a session. Cancellations 48 hours or more ahead are refunded in full. The full policy is in our Terms.
