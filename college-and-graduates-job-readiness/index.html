The distance between graduating and getting hired is not luck.

You did the degree. You cleared the exams. Then the market asks a question your marksheet doesn't answer: what can you actually do?

Employers hire evidence: of skills, of judgement, of work you've actually done. Most graduates were never shown how to build that evidence, or even how to see what's missing. That gap is not a verdict on you. It's an information problem, and information problems can be closed.

A coach who knows you. A platform that keeps you honest.

The coaching gives you judgement

A real person who knows your situation, calls out the excuse you're making, and adjusts the plan when your life changes.

The platform gives you evidence

Assessment, benchmarking and a readiness score you can watch move, so progress is measured, not assumed.

Neither works as well alone, which is the whole reason we do both.

From graduating to ready, in four steps

  1. Know yourself, choose a direction

    A structured assessment of your aptitude, interests and strengths, turned into two or three career directions worth examining, with the trade-offs stated plainly.

  2. Build what's missing

    Learning paths aimed at your gaps, plus real interview and exam preparation: mock interviews, practice questions and role-based drills.

  3. Prove you're ready

    A job-readiness score you can watch move, competencies benchmarked against real roles, and recognised certifications.

  4. Become visible to employers

    A validated, job-ready profile that hiring partners can find. The interview is still yours to win.

Is this you?

This is for you if

  • You're in your final year and can feel placement season coming.
  • You graduated and then stalled.
  • You keep reaching the interview and not the offer.
  • You suspect the problem was never your ability, but a readiness nobody taught you to build.

This is not for you if

  • You want a guaranteed job. We build readiness and visibility; we don't sell offers.

  • You want a placement agency. We're not paid by employers, which is why our advice stays honest.

What we won't promise you

  • We won't guarantee a job. We build readiness and visibility. The offer depends on an interview only you can win.
  • We won't predict your future. An assessment shows where you stand today and what's worth building.
  • We won't claim a career is "AI-proof". Nobody knows that. We work on adaptability, the honest answer to a future no one can see.
  • We won't diagnose you. We're not clinicians. If something needs clinical support, we'll say so and help you find it.

Questions

Whose platform is this? Did you build the technology?

No, and we won't pretend we did. The coaching is ours. The platform is Bodhami's, a national award-winning career-management and skill-development platform listed under Startup India (Government of India). We chose proven national infrastructure over a homemade tool. What we add is the thing a platform can't: a coach who knows your situation and holds you to the work.

Is the assessment scientifically valid?

The assessments run on Bodhami's psychometric engine, built for exactly this purpose. What we can say without qualification: no assessment anywhere predicts your future. It shows you where you stand today and what's worth building. It structures your decision. It doesn't make it.

Can you get me a job? Do you guarantee placement?

No, and please be careful with anyone who says yes. We make you genuinely ready and genuinely visible to employers. We don't sell offers, we're not paid to place you, and we won't promise an outcome that depends on an interview only you can win.

How is this different from just buying the platform, or a free online course?

A platform can tell you your gaps. It can't sit with you when you're demoralised, call out the excuse you're making, or adjust the plan when your life changes. That's the coach. The platform without coaching is a dashboard; coaching without the platform is advice you can't measure.

What does it cost?

The 20-minute call is free. The Career Clarity Session is ₹3,500, credited toward the Readiness Programme (₹12,500, 12 weeks) if you enrol within 30 days. The one-to-one intensive with Dr. Yanthan is scoped and quoted in a short conversation.

What happens to my data?

Your assessment data is processed on Bodhami's platform under their data terms, and it belongs to you. We don't sell or share it with advertisers. We don't publish your name, photo or results without your written consent. See our Privacy notice.

Do you work online, or only in Dimapur?

Our office is in Dimapur, where we coach in person. We also work online with students and job-seekers across Nagaland and North-East India. We coach in English.
