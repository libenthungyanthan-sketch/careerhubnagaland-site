Our services

We provide career assessments, career counselling, study-skills coaching and related programmes for students, graduates and schools. Fees for each service are listed on the Fees page, or quoted in writing for programmes priced on request.

What we do and do not promise

We provide guidance based on structured assessments and professional judgement. We do not guarantee admissions, examination results, scholarships or employment. Assessments describe a student's strengths and interests at the time they are taken; they do not predict future outcomes. Decisions remain with the student and family.

Booking and payment

  • A booking is confirmed when payment is received. We hold a slot for 24 hours after sending the payment link.
  • We accept UPI, cards and net banking through a secure payment link. Programmes may be paid in instalments where we offer them in writing.
  • For students under 18, a signed parental consent form is required before any assessment or session.

Rescheduling, cancellation and refunds

  • You may reschedule once, free of charge, up to 24 hours before the session.
  • Cancel 48 hours or more before the session for a full refund.
  • Cancel less than 48 hours before: we will reschedule, or refund 50%.
  • If you do not attend without notice, the fee is not refunded.
  • Once the online assessment has been started, the assessment fee is not refundable, because the platform charge has been incurred.
  • Programmes: a full refund within 7 days of enrolment if no session has taken place; after that, a refund for unused sessions within the first 4 weeks.
  • If we have to cancel, you choose a new time or a full refund.
  • School programmes follow the terms in each school's agreement.
  • Refunds are made to the original payment method within 7 working days.
