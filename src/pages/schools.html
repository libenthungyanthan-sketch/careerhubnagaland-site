Formats you can combine

Each session works for 24 to 50 students. Most schools combine a seminar for the whole grade with assessments and individual counselling for those who want it.

FormatDurationWhat it covers
Career seminar90 minutesHow careers, streams and entrances connect; how to decide well. For a full grade.
Workshop120–150 minutesHands-on activities on strengths, study habits or decision-making, in groups.
Psychometric assessmentAbout 30 minutes per studentOnline aptitude, interest and personality assessment, with an individual report.
Individual counselling30–45 minutes per studentOne-to-one results session and next steps.
Parent orientation15–30 minutesWhat the programme does, how consent works and how to support the decision at home.

How a school programme runs

  1. Planning call

    We agree the grades, formats and dates with your principal or counsellor.

  2. Proposal and consent

    You receive a written proposal and quote, and parental consent forms to send home.

  3. Delivery on campus

    Seminars, workshops and assessments at your school, with travel within Dimapur included.

  4. Reports and follow-up

    Individual reports within 24 hours, a summary for the school, and individual counselling for students who want it.
