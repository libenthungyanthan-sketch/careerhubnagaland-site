How booking and payment work

  1. Pay online, or ask first

    Choose "Pay and book" to pay securely through Razorpay (UPI, card or net banking) and tell us your preferred date. Or send an enquiry and we will send you a payment link.

  2. We confirm a time

    Usually within a few hours, on WhatsApp. Razorpay emails your receipt.

  3. Consent and preparation

    For students under 18, a parent signs the consent form. We send what to bring.

Reschedule once free of charge up to 24 hours before your session. Cancellations 48 hours or more ahead are refunded in full. See the full cancellation and refund terms.
